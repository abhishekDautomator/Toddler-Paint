Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Toddler Color Magic (the "App") treats sensitive user and device data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Toddler Color Magic is built as a completely offline coloring book application. We do not collect, store, or share any personal, sensitive, or device information from our users. Specifically:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any identifiers.
  • No Device Information: We do not collect device IDs, IP addresses, or hardware specifications.
  • No Location Data: We do not request or access your device's location.
  • No Third-Party Services: The App does not contain any ads, analytics toolkits, or third-party integration libraries that could track user behavior.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Because our App is designed for toddlers and young children, we are committed to protecting children's privacy. Our App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Since we do not collect any personal data from anyone (including children under the age of 13), there is no risk of collecting or using children's data inappropriately.

3. Data Security

Since the App operates completely locally on your device and does not transmit data over the internet, your coloring progress and usage remain entirely private on your device.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any changes are effective immediately upon posting on this page.
